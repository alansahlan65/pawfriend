## `hero-milo.png` prompt

```text
Use case: photorealistic-natural
Asset type: transparent landing-page hero pet cutout
Primary request: Create a joyful medium-sized mixed-breed rescue dog named Milo, tan and white, photographed as a fresh standalone subject.
Scene/backdrop: genuinely transparent background with a clean alpha channel; no floor, no shadow plane, no colored halo, no backdrop.
Subject: one real-looking tan-and-white rescue dog with a white blaze and chest, large expressive asymmetrical upright ears, warm brown eyes, open happy mouth, friendly energetic personality.
Style/medium: premium editorial studio pet photography, natural fur detail, believable anatomy, authentic rather than glossy stock photography.
Composition/framing: straight-on close crop, chest-up, centered, both ears fully inside frame, face dominant, slight wide-angle intimacy but no distortion, complete silhouette suitable for responsive web layering.
Lighting/mood: soft natural studio daylight, gentle contrast, warm optimistic mood, crisp eyes and whiskers, subtle dimensional rim separation.
Constraints: high resolution; isolated subject only; preserve fine fur edges in transparency; one dog; no collar with readable brand; no text; no logo; no watermark.
Avoid: opaque or checkerboard background, fake transparency, floor shadow, props, humans, extra animals, cropped ears, cut-off muzzle, costumes, illustration, painterly style, plastic fur, excessive retouching.
```
