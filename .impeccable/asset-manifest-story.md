# PawFriend story asset manifest

Five source PNGs were generated with the built-in image generation tool. They were visually inspected for humane treatment, natural anatomy, crop suitability, and the absence of readable logos, text, and watermarks. Their exact generation prompts are embedded in each PNG as a `tEXt` chunk.

The page delivers optimized WebP assets. Most map directly to a documented PNG source; two delivery-only files remain in the current workspace without a source PNG or embedded prompt and must not be described as newly generated from this manifest.

## `luna-shelter.png`

- **Path:** `public/images/story/luna-shelter.png`
- **Purpose:** Opening image in Luna's before-and-after adoption success story: safe, cared for, and quietly waiting in a humane shelter.
- **Dimensions:** 1120 x 1400 px (4:5 portrait)
- **Prompt:**

```text
Use case: photorealistic-natural
Asset type: PawFriend animal-shelter landing page success-story photograph
Primary request: A warm editorial photograph of Luna, a brown mackerel tabby cat, in a clean humane shelter enclosure, safe, healthy, and visibly well cared for while quietly waiting for adoption.
Scene/backdrop: Bright orderly independent animal shelter cat room, clean cream bedding, subtle glass-front enclosure details, calm uncluttered environment.
Subject: One adult brown tabby cat with green-amber eyes and a small cream chin, sitting near the front and looking gently toward the camera; natural alert posture, no signs of fear or distress.
Style/medium: Photorealistic natural-light editorial animal photography, tactile film grain, honest documentary warmth, premium nonprofit campaign imagery.
Composition/framing: Portrait 4:5 crop, cat centered slightly off-axis, eye-level camera, enough environmental context to read as a humane shelter, clean edges for responsive web cropping.
Lighting/mood: Soft window light, warm cream and muted brown palette, patient, dignified, hopeful, quiet.
Constraints: A safe cared-for animal waiting for a home; realistic anatomy and fur; no people; no text; no logos; no watermark.
Avoid: Distress, sadness cues, dirty cages, neglect, harsh wire bars dominating the frame, medical equipment, commercial branding, collars with readable tags, oversaturated colors, illustration or CGI.
```
