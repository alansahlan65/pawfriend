# PawFriend Surface Brief

## Outcome

Persuade prospective adopters that PawFriend helps them find a personality match, not browse inventory. The primary action is “Meet the pets,” followed by comparing all nine residents, saving a private shortlist, and preparing for a thoughtful visit. Profile details and availability are confirmation-sensitive; the page does not use invented totals, addresses, outcomes, or contact details as proof.

## Approved Direction

- Reference: `.impeccable/mocks/pawfriend-comp-b-approved.png`
- Visual world: tactile adoption zine crossed with a portrait contact sheet.
- Product thesis: every pet is a person, not inventory.
- First viewport: exact three-line headline, Milo portrait, handwritten personality note, proof cluster, and one dominant adoption CTA.
- Story: emotional introduction → featured residents → complete catalog → personality fit → adoption journey → patient match → visitor preparation → FAQ → mission → final invitation.

## Surface Inventory

- Component grammar: editorial bands, pinned contact-sheet travel, imperfect photo cards, large portrait cutouts, black CTA blocks, handwritten notes, a full catalog grid, and an ink-and-lime shortlist drawer.
- Corner language: mostly square; circles are reserved for icon controls and portrait proof.
- Line weights: 1px editorial rules, 2px icon strokes, and heavy condensed display type.
- Elevation: shallow physical-paper shadows and restrained cutout drop shadows only.
- Type: self-hosted Anton display, Archivo body and utility, Georgia editorial serif, and restrained Caveat annotations.
- Colors: recycled paper `#F5EDDE`, ink `#121314`, safety orange `#EF6C1B`, adoption lime `#D9EF42`, soft blue `#A9D4EE`, and warm pink `#EFA4B4`.
- Assets: warm resident portraits, transparent pet cutouts, and documentary journey photography with durable provenance manifests.
- Signature components: Milo hero portrait, tilted resident contact prints, nine-resident catalog, save notifications, focus-managed shortlist dialog, paw-based personality matrix, and connected adoption journey.
- Motion: asynchronously loaded GSAP/ScrollTrigger powers two fine-pointer desktop handoffs: the hero-to-introduction page rise and the sideways personality-to-journey reveal. Resident travel supports the story. Touch and reduced-motion layouts stay in normal flow.
- Metadata: canonical URLs, social cards, JSON-LD, conditional indexing, sitemap, manifest, and policy pages ship with the surface.

## Finish Contract

Keep the exact headline “Find your new best friend.” Preserve the one-realistic-portrait-per-story-beat rhythm and the obvious “Meet the pets” outcome. The resident catalog, favorites, notifications, shortlist dialog, share fallbacks, anchors, FAQ, and policy links must work. Avoid gradients, excessive rounding, glass effects, unverified claims, or decorative motion that competes with the pets. Desktop, touch, keyboard, high-contrast, storage-failure, and reduced-motion paths must remain complete.
