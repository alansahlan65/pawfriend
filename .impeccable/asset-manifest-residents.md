- Pet: Milo, tan-and-white mixed-breed dog with alert upright ears
- Dimensions: 1254 × 1254 px
- Prompt:

```text
Use case: photorealistic-natural
Asset type: square resident portrait for the PawFriend animal-shelter landing page
Primary request: Create a fresh standalone editorial shelter portrait of Milo, a tan-and-white mixed-breed rescue dog with large alert upright ears and a friendly, attentive expression.
Scene/backdrop: Quiet neutral cream shelter or foster-home interior, softly blurred with no identifiable signage or objects.
Subject: One dog only; tan coat with a clean white muzzle, chest, and narrow forehead blaze; natural healthy fur texture; soulful brown eyes; realistic mixed-breed proportions.
Style/medium: Warm, authentic editorial animal photography, subtle analog-film character, realistic rather than polished commercial stock.
Composition/framing: Square crop, eye level, face and shoulders visible, centered with comfortable breathing room around both ears, direct gaze toward camera, shallow depth of field, 85mm portrait feel.
Lighting/mood: Soft warm natural window light, gentle shadows, welcoming and hopeful.
Color palette: Warm tan, ivory, muted beige, restrained natural color.
Constraints: One subject per image; standalone photograph only; preserve realistic anatomy, eyes, fur, nose, and ears; background must remain quiet and softly blurred; no frame or collage.
Avoid: text, letters, numbers, logos, watermark, labels, clothing, costumes, human hands, cages, exaggerated grin, distorted anatomy, duplicate animals, harsh studio lighting.
```
